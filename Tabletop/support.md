# Tabletop: Face to Face Games — Support

Tabletop is a set of games for two people (or two teams) sharing one device: Draw & Guess,
Fleet Hunt, Air Hockey, Checkers, Four in a Row, and Dots and Boxes.

## Which games work on my device?

- **iPhone Duo and iPad:** all six games.
- **Other iPhones:** Air Hockey, Checkers, Four in a Row, and Dots and Boxes. Draw & Guess and
  Fleet Hunt need a larger screen and show as "iPhone Duo & iPad" on the home screen.
  Phone-to-phone play for those games is planned for a future update.

## How do I play on iPhone Duo?

Each game tells you how to hold the phone the first time you open it.

- **Lay it flat** for Air Hockey, Checkers, Four in a Row, and Dots and Boxes: one player at each
  end, the crease is the middle of the table.
- **Draw & Guess:** stand the phone up like a laptop to peek at your word (only you can see it),
  then open it flat and draw for everyone.
- **Fleet Hunt:** place your fleet, then pass the phone. A full-screen "Pass to…" card keeps the
  other player's board hidden until they tap.

If a game shows "Open your phone flat" or "Turn your phone upright," change the pose and the game
continues where it left off.

## Common questions

**The game paused when I closed the phone.** That is intended. Draw & Guess pauses its clock when
the phone closes and resumes when you open it. Other games wait for you to open it flat.

**Can I turn off sounds or vibration?** Yes, in a future update there will be a settings screen;
for now the phone's silent switch mutes all game sounds.

**Checkers lets me skip a capture.** By default captures are optional (the piece that can capture
glows orange as a hint). Turn on "Forced captures" on the Checkers setup screen for the
tournament rule.

**Can I rename the players?** Yes, on each game's setup screen. Names are remembered.

**Does the app need the internet?** No. Everything is on the device.
