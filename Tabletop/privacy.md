# Tabletop: Face to Face Games — Privacy Policy

**Effective:** October 23, 2026
**Developer:** JDR3AM Studios

Tabletop is a collection of games played on one device by people in the same room. It is built
to work without collecting anything about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. Tabletop does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- **No network use.** The app does not connect to the internet. Word lists, sounds, and all game
  logic are included in the app itself.
- **No ads.** Tabletop is a paid app with no advertising.

## What stays on your device

The app saves a small amount of settings on your device so it remembers your choices between
sessions: player names you type in, your game options (for example grid size, run length, which
color you picked), sound and haptic preferences, and which first-run tips you have already seen.
This information is stored only on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when you
delete the app.

## Children

Tabletop does not collect information from anyone, including children. The "kid-safe words"
option in Draw & Guess limits the word list to simpler words; it does not change what the app
collects, which is nothing.

## Hardware features

On iPhone Duo, Tabletop reads the hinge position (open, partially open, closed) to lay out the
screen for the way you are holding the phone. This information is used only in the moment to
choose a layout and is not stored or transmitted.

## Future features

If a later version adds optional online play (for example through Apple's Game Center), this
policy will be updated before that feature is released, and that feature will be opt-in.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective
date.
